- On Android the keyboard stays open when you pull down the notification shade and close it again
- Database drivers stay installed when the server updates, instead of having to be installed again
- On the phone, Chat background applies a background as soon as you tap it, and its settings open on top in a dialog of their own, as on the desktop
- Restoring a backup, from a file or from Google Drive, switches to its environment and shows its default or locked project in the chat list
- The transfers panel and the undo notice sit above the navigation bar on the phone, a quick flick dismisses a notice, unlocking with the security key in Terminal, Databases or Project files unlocks the other two as well, the PC monitor's cards fill the width on servers without a GPU, and the same chat open in two environments no longer jumps to the other tab when you change its project
