- Chats can be sorted into categories, and both chats and categories are reordered by dragging them where you want them
- Turning on the trash makes a deleted chat go there instead of disappearing, to be read and restored later
- A chat can be moved to another project, and folders are chosen from a picker instead of typed
- Forms take more kinds of answers: sliders, colours, bars, file and path fields, and sections or pages to fold a long form
- Asking what the session is spending marks the models that have not been used
- The context ring follows what the session is really spending, and lands on the new size as soon as a compaction ends
- Every chat in the list says what it is doing — working, waiting, compacting or failed — from the moment it starts
- The web app has its own address for each screen, so the back button and a reload leave you where you were
- Images open at full resolution, zoom where you point with the wheel, a double tap or a pinch, and are dragged around freely
- Reopening a compacted conversation shows the compaction again, with its summary, instead of an empty chat
- The chat no longer shifts when a block is opened or closed, and the header of an open block stays in place while it scrolls
- Smaller fixes across the interface: dialogs, popup menus, scrollbars, drag and drop, timestamps and text fields, with both apps kept in step
