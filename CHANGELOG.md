- ACP agents in the side panel chats with Copilot, Codex, Cursor or any agent you install from the catalog in the Server settings, with its own models, modes, commands and sessions
- Claude hands tasks to the ACP agents you turn on and to the accounts marked Claude can use it instead of a Delegation section, picks the model they run with, and the chat shows who answers
- The side panel's views reorder by dragging, and move into its menu or back out, on the phone too
- Sessions in the Server settings sets how long a persistent chat or an ACP agent stays open after you leave, and how many stay open at once
- Packs show what Use puts on in a banner drawn with their colours, and their picture as an icon
- On the desktop and Android the app tells you once when a new version is out
- Rewinding a chat updates it on your other devices, Update restarts a server you already updated from the terminal, links in mods ask before opening like those in the chat, the logo takes each theme's colours, and a notice tells you when an environment's token is not accepted
