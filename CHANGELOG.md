- Claude can now use a browser that runs in the right panel: it opens pages, reads them and takes screenshots while you watch, and you can take the mouse and keyboard at any moment — with its own tabs, back and forward, a device picker and a pick-an-element mode
- Settings has a search box: type what you want and it lists the matching options with their current value, whatever their section, and takes you to it — opening the right dialog when the option lives inside one. It reaches the tools, the keyboard shortcuts, your environments, your accounts and the permission modes too
- A **Privacy** section decides what leaves this machine: whether a chat started here can be taken over from claude.ai, whether Claude signs its commits as co-author, and whether conversations are uploaded to Anthropic. Each one can be left as Claude Code's own default
- Generation gained the reply language, thinking before every answer, and automatic compaction; chats can now be kept forever instead of expiring, and the Agent SDK updates itself on start or on demand, with its changelog readable from the app
- An account can point at an address of your own with an API key, and Anthropic and OmniRoute come preset — the model list is read from the provider, and nothing is pinned for you
- The server updates itself from Settings: it fetches, tells you how many changes are waiting, and applies them with a rebase — saying plainly when your copy is modified or when the server has to be restarted for them to take effect
- The local server tells you what it is doing — running, starting, stopped, or running outside CConnect — and can be started, restarted or stopped from the app without leaving it
- Dictate your message instead of typing it: the microphone next to Send writes what you say into the composer, on the phone, the desktop app and the browser
- Attach, commands and the rest moved into a single **+** menu, so the composer bar keeps two buttons and the chat controls stay readable
- Files attached from the file manager now show as a chip instead of a raw path, both while the chat runs and when you come back to it
- Keyboard shortcuts, the command palette and the settings sections share one look, dialog scrollbars sit at the dialog edge, and the search fields across the app are all the same height
