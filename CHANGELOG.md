- The right panel is no longer only a terminal: from its own header you can put a chat, your files, Claude, the monitor, a terminal or the scratchpad in it, and the sections that used to take over the window — Claude's plugins, skills and MCP servers, or your SSH hosts — now open inside the panel, so whatever you were reading on the left stays where it was
- Two chats side by side are finally independent: each keeps its own environment colour throughout, selecting all only takes the panel you are working in, and typing goes to the chat you last touched
- With your files open next to a conversation, dragging one onto the chat attaches it, and the menu offers the same thing. Nothing is uploaded — the file is already on your PC — so attaching a recording or an installer is as immediate as attaching a screenshot
- A command box on Ctrl+K searches your chats, projects, screens, settings and every available action, each with its keyboard shortcut beside it
- Every keyboard shortcut can be reassigned from settings, and the app tells you when the keys you chose are already taken
- On desktop, settings opens as a dialog with its sections down the side instead of covering the app, and it now holds everything about Claude: the CLI, your plan usage and your accounts
- The window opens sized to your screen the first time and afterwards remembers how big it was, where you left it and whether it was maximised
- Your SSH hosts are reachable from the terminal panel itself, and the parts that only work on the desktop app no longer appear in the web version
- When the server cannot be reached, every screen says so instead of spinning forever, and the actions that need it are disabled rather than failing when you press them
- The chat list starts open on desktop, and dragging a file with the mouse no longer asks you to select it first
- Fields with buttons in them are no longer taller than the rest, the colour circle of an environment is centred, and menus, empty states and separators look the same wherever they appear
- Copying now works in the web version, the changelog opens as a dialog, and asking for your plan usage no longer breaks when a request is refused
